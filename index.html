Write a second Voynich

The Voynichizer writes a second Voynich manuscript. Give it a text and a key: it composes a new book of 207 folios, page by page, with the vocabulary, the word shapes, the lines, the paragraphs and the sections of the real one, a Voynich II made to be indistinguishable from the original. Hidden in its word counts is your text: with the same key it comes back exactly; without it, there is nothing to read.

The limit is in bits, not characters: about 10,000 bytes once compressed, i.e. some 20,000–25,000 characters of ordinary prose, more if the text is repetitive, fewer in scripts that take more bytes per character.
Use a long passphrase, four words or more. Without it the text cannot be read back, and nobody can recover it for you.

More options

Everything happens in your browser: your text and your key never leave this computer. The first time, the page fetches about 27 MB (Python and its libraries); writing a book takes a few minutes.

On a phone this may not work: writing a book needs about 500 MB of memory. A computer is better.

How close the copy comes is measured, not claimed: two classifiers trained to tell its pages from the real ones score 0.56 and 0.61 over 24 keys, where 0.5 is a coin toss and 1 a perfect judge. See what it has in common with the Voynich and the measurements.
